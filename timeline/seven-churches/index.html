Ephesus

Revelation 2:1-7Apostolic Zeal Era (A.D. 31-100)
Name MeaningDesirable, lovely

Presentation of Christ

Christ walks among the seven lampstands and holds the seven stars in His hand, showing His immediate care for His churches and His authority over their messengers.

Commendation

Labor, patience, doctrinal discernment, and hatred of the deeds of the Nicolaitans

Warning

Lost first love

Spiritual Risk and Era Context

Truthful labor can outlive deep devotion. Ephesus had zeal, endurance, and discernment, yet Christ exposed a loss of first love that threatened the heart of its witness.

Divine Counsel

Remember from where you have fallen, repent, and do the first works.

Promise to the Overcomer

Access to the tree of life in the paradise of God
